TechnoBotz FTC 23542

TechnoBotz Nonprofit is a competitive robotics team of middle and high school students in Frisco, Texas. We compete in the FIRST Tech Challenge, a robotics competition for grades 7 to 12 run by the nonprofit FIRST. FIRST LEGO League since 2021, FTC since 2023.
